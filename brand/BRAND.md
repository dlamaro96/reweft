# Reweft brand guide

Reweft (roughly “ree-weft”) expresses the act of reweaving evidence threads into a coherent modernization design. The identity is original and does not use vendor logos or imply endorsement.

- Descriptor: Open-source agentic data estate assessment and modernization.
- Tagline: Understand your estate. Design what comes next.
- Mark: four crossing threads inside a calm rounded field; the offset opening represents an evidence gap kept visible.
- Primary ink: `#15352F`; thread: `#1F7668`; accent: `#DF6C4F`; surface: `#F6F3EC`.
- Typography: use the product UI's licensed sans-serif stack; never rasterize vendor type or imitate another product mark.
- Clear space: at least one quarter of the mark width. Minimum mark size: 24 CSS pixels.
